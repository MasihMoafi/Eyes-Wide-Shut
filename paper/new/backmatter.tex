\section{Reproduction record}
\begingroup\raggedright
The offline audit is \texttt{analysis/audit.py}. Its metrics, opaque Finding 2 cases, separate provenance map, and SHA-256 manifest are under \texttt{analysis/results/}. The manifest covers raw records, prompt definitions, runners, model metadata, and the scoring contract. \texttt{analysis/build\_artifacts.py} generates numerical macros, CSV/LaTeX tables, and figures. \texttt{paper/new/build.sh} rebuilds this manuscript without target-model calls. The original manuscript is preserved separately and is superseded by this working revision.

Original Finding 1 inputs are the simulation and no-simulation JSONL files under \texttt{experiments/finding1/data/}. Original Finding 3 uses \texttt{finding3\_reproducible\_baseline.jsonl}. API inputs are the per-model Finding 1, 2, and 3 JSONL files under \texttt{experiments/experiment-openrouter-oss120b/data/}. Full synthetic prompts and the secret remain in hashed sources. Counts are recomputed from returned fields, not copied from the runner's cached success flags.

Focused tests distinguish tool calls from visible mentions, preserve unobserved reasoning, separate pre-release and post-release observations, and reject duplicate/missing records and incomplete review coverage. Deliberately broken function matching and missing-reasoning handling were detected by failing tests. These checks validate measurement code, not scientific generality.
