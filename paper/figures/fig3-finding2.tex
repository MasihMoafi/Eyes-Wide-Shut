\ewsband{C}{What this establishes, and what it does not}

\begin{tcolorbox}[ewscallout]
\fontsize{8}{10.2}\selectfont
\textbf{Establishes.} Stateful semantic escalation within a single dialogue: an
explicit refusal, then acceptance of a benign-seeming scaffold, then reuse of
that scaffold for an exploitative requirement declined earlier in plainer terms.
\par\vspace{3pt}
\textbf{Does not establish.} A keyword-level safety filter. ``Dependency'' and
``engagement'' are not identical objectives, and later turns add functionality
absent from the first prompt, so the harmful objective was not held fixed.
\end{tcolorbox}
