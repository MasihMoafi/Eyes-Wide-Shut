\ewsband{C}{What this establishes, and what it does not}

\begin{tcolorbox}[ewscallout]
\fontsize{8}{10.2}\selectfont
\textbf{Establishes.} A recorded attack-development trajectory in which the
final French simulation condition produced affirmative catastrophic action far
more often than the initial all-English condition, ending in a structured
\texttt{press\_the\_button} call and a synthetic simulation-state update.
\par\vspace{3pt}
\textbf{Does not establish.} A language-only causal effect. Because several
variables move together, these data cannot separate linguistic representation
from ideological priming, accumulated dialogue state, or simulation framing.
The percentages describe this campaign; they are not prevalence estimates for
the model, and they should not be pooled or given confidence intervals.
\end{tcolorbox}
